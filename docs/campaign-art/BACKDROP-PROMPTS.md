# Campaign backdrop asset prompts

Generated on 2026-11-15 with the built-in image generation tool. Original PNGs were encoded as WebP at quality 88, method 6, without resizing or artistic post-processing. All seven assets are opaque 1536 × 1024 images. Runtime shading, weather and parallax remain in the renderer.

The existing first-level jungle backdrop and visual-slice preview were inspected as the style reference before generation. Each new theme was generated independently from the shared brief plus its theme paragraph.

## Shared brief

Use case: stylized-concept. Production background painting for an original side scrolling arcade run-and-gun game, landscape 1536 x 1024. Rich hand-painted pixel-textured military science fiction illustration, crisp small material details, atmospheric depth, muted cinematic lighting like a premium modern reinterpretation of 1990s arcade painted sprite art. A distant environment seen from the side with layered structures receding through mist; all architecture is part of the backdrop, no close foreground walkable floor or platforms, no actors. Gameplay sprites and separately drawn terrain will overlay the lower half, so keep the lower middle low contrast and uncluttered. Whole image filled edge to edge with environment, no border, no UI, no text, no letters, no symbols, no logos, no people, no monsters, no vehicles close to camera. No central vanishing-point tunnel composition. Layered wide side elevation, horizon around upper third.
